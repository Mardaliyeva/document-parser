# Code of conduct

## Our standard

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free community. Contributors should
communicate respectfully, accept constructive feedback, focus discussion on the
project, and assume good intent while remaining accountable for their impact.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discriminatory language, personal
attacks, threats, deliberate intimidation, publishing another person's private
information, or sustained disruption of project spaces.

## Scope

This policy applies in the repository, issue tracker, pull requests, discussions,
and public spaces where a person represents the project.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through GitHub. Do not
open a public issue containing personal or sensitive information. The maintainer
may remove content, issue a warning, temporarily restrict participation, or ban a
participant when necessary to protect the community.

Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical. Retaliation against a
person who reports a concern in good faith is not permitted.
